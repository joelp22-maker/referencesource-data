**Provision:** (c)(4)(iii)

**Applies to:** shipments with packages required to display the paragraph (c)(3)(i) lithium battery mark

**Requirement:** Each shipment must include an indication on the air waybill of compliance with this paragraph (c)(4) (or the applicable ICAO Technical Instructions Packing Instruction).

> Each shipment with packages required to display the paragraph (c)(3)(i) lithium battery mark must include an indication on the air waybill of compliance with this <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/section-173.185#p-173.185(c)(4)> paragraph (c)(4) (or the applicable ICAO Technical Instructions Packing Instruction), when an air waybill is used.
