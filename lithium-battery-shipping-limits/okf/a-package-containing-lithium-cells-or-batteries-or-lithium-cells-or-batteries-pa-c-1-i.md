**Provision:** (c)(1)(i)

**Applies to:** a package containing lithium cells or batteries, or lithium cells or batteries packed with, or contained in, equipment

**Threshold:** 20 Wh

**Requirement:** The Watt-hour (Wh) rating may not exceed 20 Wh for a lithium ion cell

> ( i ) The Watt-hour (Wh) rating may not exceed 20 Wh for a lithium ion cell or 100 Wh for a lithium ion battery.
