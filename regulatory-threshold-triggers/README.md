# US federal employment law thresholds by employee count

Employee-count thresholds that trigger US federal employment laws (FMLA, ADA, COBRA, Title VII, ACA, ERISA and more). Each record is one law at one threshold, stating the trigger point, what the employer is required to do, and how employees are counted for that statute — including whether part-time, remote or FTE counts apply. Answers 'how many employees before [law] applies' and 'does FMLA count remote workers within 75 miles'.

Canonical, always-current version: https://referencesource.org/regulatory-threshold-triggers/
Machine-readable: https://referencesource.org/regulatory-threshold-triggers/data.json

Every value carries the source URL it came from and a verbatim quote
from that source. Records are individually addressable at
`https://referencesource.org/regulatory-threshold-triggers/<record-id>/`.
