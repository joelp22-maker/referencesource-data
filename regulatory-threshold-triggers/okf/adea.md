**Law:** ADEA

**Full name:** Age Discrimination in Employment Act

**Employee threshold:** 20

**Requirement:** you cannot discriminate against (or harass) employees or applicants age 40 or older simply due to their age

**Counting note:** part-time and temporary employees are included in the head-count calculation

> Age Discrimination in Employment Act (ADEA) Per the <https://www.eeoc.gov/eeoc/publications/age.cfm> Age Discrimination in Employment Act (ADEA), you cannot discriminate against (or harass) employees or applicants age 40 or older simply due to their age.
