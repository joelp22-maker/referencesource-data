**Law:** Affirmative Action

**Full name:** Affirmative Action Plans (AAPs)

**Employee threshold:** 50

**Requirement:** Organizations with 50 or more employees and $50,000 in government contracts must have an AAP

> Affirmative Action Affirmative action plans (AAPs) define a company’s policies and procedures on training programs, outreach efforts, and business practices that promote qualified minorities, women, people with disabilities, and veterans for equal employment opportunities. Organizations with 50 or more employees and $50,000 in government contracts must have an AAP.
