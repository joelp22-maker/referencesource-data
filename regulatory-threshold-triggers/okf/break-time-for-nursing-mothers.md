**Law:** Break Time for Nursing Mothers

**Full name:** Break Time for Nursing Mothers

**Employee threshold:** 1

**Requirement:** you to provide rest breaks and accommodations (in a location other than a bathroom) for nursing mothers to express breast milk for up to 1 year after the child’s birth

**Counting note:** Employers with fewer than 50 employees may be exempt if they can demonstrate an undue hardship

> Break Time for Nursing Mothers <https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/fact-sheets/73-flsa-break-time-nursing-mothers> Break Time for Nursing Mothers is a sub-section to the ACA that requires you to provide rest breaks and accommodations (in a location other than a bathroom) for nursing mothers to express breast milk for up to 1 year after the child’s birth.
