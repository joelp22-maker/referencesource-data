**Law:** GINA

**Full name:** Genetic Information Nondiscrimination Act

**Employee threshold:** 15

**Requirement:** you cannot discriminate against employees or applicants based on their genetic information or family medical history

**Counting note:** part-time and temporary employees are included in the head-count calculation

> Genetic Information Nondiscrimination Act (GINA) According to the <https://www.eeoc.gov/eeoc/publications/fs-gina.cfm> Genetic Information Nondiscrimination Act (GINA), you cannot discriminate against employees or applicants based on their genetic information or family medical history.
