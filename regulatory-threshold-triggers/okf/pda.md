**Law:** PDA

**Full name:** Pregnancy Discrimination Act

**Employee threshold:** 15

**Requirement:** requires you to treat employees who are pregnant or recently went through childbirth the same as any other applicant or employee regarding their ability or inability to work

**Counting note:** part-time and temporary employees are included in the head-count calculation

> Pregnancy Discrimination Act (PDA) The <https://www.eeoc.gov/eeoc/publications/fs-preg.cfm> Pregnancy Discrimination Act (PDA) requires you to treat employees who are pregnant or recently went through childbirth the same as any other applicant or employee regarding their ability or inability to work.
