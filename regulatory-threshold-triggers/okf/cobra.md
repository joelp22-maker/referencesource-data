**Law:** COBRA

**Full name:** Consolidated Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act

**Employee threshold:** 20

**Requirement:** requires employers to offer continuation coverage of employer-sponsored group health insurance to eligible employees and their dependents when coverage would otherwise be lost due to certain events such as termination

**Counting note:** part-time and temporary employees are included in the head-count calculation
