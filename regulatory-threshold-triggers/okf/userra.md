> Uniformed Services Employment and Reemployment Rights Act (USERRA) The <https://www.dol.gov/agencies/vets/programs/userra/USERRA%20Pocket%20Guide> Uniformed Services Employment and Reemployment Rights Act (USERRA) says that when an employee returns from military service or training, you are required to provide them with either their former job or a comparable job to what they had prior to military duty.
