**Item category:** Fire Extinguishers (UN1044) — Division 2.2 nonflammable compressed gas

**Carrier:** USPS

**Restriction type:** restricted

**Scope:** domestic mail

**Conditions:** Mailable if do not contain methyl bromide gas mixtures, meet DOT container specifications, hold nonflammable/nonpoisonous/noncorrosive gas; only one extinguisher per mailpiece

> Fire Extinguishers (UN1044): Division 2.2 nonflammable compressed gas. Fire extinguishers that contain nonflammable compressed gas are mailable if they do not contain methyl bromide gas mixtures
