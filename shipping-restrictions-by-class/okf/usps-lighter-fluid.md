**Item category:** Lighter fluid

**Carrier:** USPS

**Restriction type:** restricted

**Scope:** domestic mail via surface transportation only

**Conditions:** Permitted only via surface transportation if fluid qualifies as Limited Quantity surface material and is within quantity limitations and packaging requirements; Packaging Instruction 3A must be followed

> Lighter Fluid: Considered a flammable liquid and typically has a flashpoint between 20&deg; F (-7&deg; C) and 55&deg; F (13&deg; C). It is permitted only in domestic mail via surface transportation
