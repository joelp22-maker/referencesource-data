**Item category:** Spare (uninstalled) lithium metal batteries

**Carrier:** Airlines (all US carriers)

**Restriction type:** carry-on only

**Scope:** passenger air travel

**Conditions:** Must be in carry-on baggage only; if carry-on is checked at gate or planeside, batteries must be removed and kept with passenger in cabin; battery terminals must be protected from short circuit; size limit 2 grams of lithium content per battery (up to 8 grams with airline approval, max 2 batteries); prohibited if carried for resale/distribution

> Spare (uninstalled) lithium ion and lithium metal batteries, including power banks and cell phone battery charging cases, must be carried in carry-on baggage only
