**Item category:** Cigarette lighters charged with fuel (NA1057)

**Carrier:** USPS

**Restriction type:** prohibited

**Scope:** all mail

**Conditions:** Lighters charged with fuel and having an ignition system are nonmailable; may seek DOT approval number for mailability ruling

> Cigarette Lighters (NA1057): Generally classified as Class 3 flammable liquid, lighters charged with fuel and having an ignition system (or similar heating, lighting, or ignition device) are nonmailable.
