**Item category:** Category A infectious substances (UN2814, UN2900)

**Carrier:** USPS

**Restriction type:** prohibited

**Scope:** all mail including international

**Conditions:** Category A infectious substances (capable of causing permanent disability or life-threatening disease in healthy humans/animals) are nonmailable

> Category A: An infectious substance transported in a form capable of causing permanent disability or life-threatening or fatal disease in otherwise healthy humans or animals when exposure occurs. Category A infectious substances are nonmailable.
