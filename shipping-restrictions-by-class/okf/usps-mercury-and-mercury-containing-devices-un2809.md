**Item category:** Mercury and mercury-containing devices (UN2809)

**Carrier:** USPS

**Restriction type:** prohibited

**Scope:** all mail

**Conditions:** Nonmailable; includes metallic mercury and thermometers, barometers, sphygmomanometers containing metallic mercury

> Mercury and Mercury-Containing Devices (UN2809): Includes metallic mercury and items such as thermometers, barometers, and sphygmomanometers (i.e., blood pressure meters) that contain metallic mercury.
