**Item category:** Inks (flammable or combustible liquid)

**Carrier:** USPS

**Restriction type:** restricted

**Scope:** domestic mail via surface transportation

**Conditions:** Mailable if qualify as Limited Quantity surface material and meet requirements; primary receptacles must be cushioned with absorbent material; follow Packaging Instruction 3A or 3B as applicable

> Inks: Classified as flammable or combustible liquids. Defined as colored liquids used for writing, drawing, and printing, and often composed of pigments such as carbon black suspended in a drying oil. They are mailable if they qualify as Limited Quantity surface material
