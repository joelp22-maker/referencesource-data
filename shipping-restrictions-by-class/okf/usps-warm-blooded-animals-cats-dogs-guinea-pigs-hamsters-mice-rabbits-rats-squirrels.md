**Item category:** warm-blooded animals (cats, dogs, guinea pigs, hamsters, mice, rabbits, rats, squirrels)

**Carrier:** USPS

**Restriction type:** prohibited

**Scope:** all domestic mail

**Conditions:** Warm-blooded animals, except for adult birds and specified day-old birds, are not mailable; nonmailable list includes cats or kittens, dogs or puppies, guinea pigs, hamsters, mice, rabbits, rats, squirrels and flying squirrels

> Warm-blooded animals, except for adult birds and specified day-old birds under the specific conditions in this section, are not mailable.
