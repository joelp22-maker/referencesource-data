**Item category:** Dry Ice (carbon dioxide solid)

**Carrier:** USPS

**Restriction type:** restricted

**Scope:** Domestic Mail via Air Transportation (up to 5 pounds per mailpiece) or Surface Transportation; prohibited International

**Conditions:** Via air, permitted in quantities of up to 5 pounds per mailpiece. Must comply with Packaging Instruction 9A in Appendix C. Dry ice is prohibited in international mail.
