**Item category:** Exempt human or animal specimens

**Carrier:** USPS

**Restriction type:** restricted

**Scope:** all domestic mail

**Conditions:** Not regulated as hazardous materials; must be triple-packaged per 346.327; outer packaging must be marked 'Exempt human specimen' or 'Exempt animal specimen'

> Exempt human or animal specimens as defined in 346.12 (d) , are not regulated as hazardous materials but must be packaged to meet mailing standards, including being triple-packaged
