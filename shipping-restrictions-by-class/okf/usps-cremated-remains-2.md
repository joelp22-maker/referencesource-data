**Item category:** Cremated Remains

**Carrier:** USPS

**Restriction type:** restricted

**Scope:** Domestic via Priority Mail Express only; International via Priority Mail Express International only when destination country permits

**Conditions:** Domestic: Must be sent via Priority Mail Express service using USPS-produced cremated remains packaging (BOX-CRE). International: Only through Priority Mail Express International in BOX-CRE packaging when the destination country permits. Must verify destination country accepts both service and cremated remains.
