**Item category:** Biological products exceeding 4 liters or 4 kg containing Category A

**Carrier:** USPS

**Restriction type:** prohibited

**Scope:** all mail

**Conditions:** Biological products, cultures, or specimens known or suspected to contain Category A infectious substance, or exceeding 4 liters/4 kg limits, are nonmailable

> Biological Products and Specimens: Biological products, cultures, stocks, or other biological specimens known or suspected to contain a Category A infectious substance. Mailpieces exceeding 4 liters (1 gallon) for liquids or 4 kg. (8.8 pounds) for solids are nonmailable.
