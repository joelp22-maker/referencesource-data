**Item category:** Electronic devices with lithium batteries (battery not connected)

**Carrier:** Royal Mail / Post Office

**Restriction type:** restricted

**Scope:** UK

**Conditions:** Maximum number of lithium batteries is minimum required to power device plus two spares; Wh rating must not exceed 20Wh per cell or 100Wh per battery; prohibited internationally
