**Item category:** Regulated magnetized materials — international mail

**Carrier:** USPS

**Scope:** international mail

**Conditions:** Regulated magnetized materials are prohibited from international mail; only non-regulated materials with field strength below 0.002 gauss at 7 feet may be mailed internationally

> International Mail: Only non-regulated magnetized materials with a magnetic field strength below 0.002 gauss at a distance of 7 feet may be mailed internationally. Regulated magnetized materials are prohibited
