**Item category:** small harmless cold-blooded animals (except snakes, turtles, turtle eggs)

**Carrier:** USPS

**Restriction type:** allowed

**Scope:** domestic mail

**Conditions:** Mailable only when they meet specific requirements including ability to reach destination in good condition, not requiring food or water (except fish), not creating sanitary problems or obnoxious odors; includes baby alligators, chameleons, frogs, lizards, newts, salamanders, tadpoles, goldfish, tropical fish, worms, leeches, snails, and nonpoisonous insects

> Small, harmless, cold-blooded animals, except for snakes, turtles, and turtle eggs, are mailable only when they meet requirements specified in Exhibit 526.6
