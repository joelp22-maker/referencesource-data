**Item category:** Acetone (UN1090), Benzene (UN1114), Hexane(s) (UN1208)

**Carrier:** USPS

**Restriction type:** prohibited

**Scope:** all mail

**Conditions:** Nonmailable unless diluted with less flammable or nonflammable materials sufficient to raise flashpoint to 20 F or higher

> Acetone (UN1090) normally has a flashpoint of 0&deg; F, Benzene (UN1114) normally has a flashpoint of 12&deg; F, and Hexane(s) (UN1208) normally have flashpoints in the vicinity of -15&deg; F. All are nonmailable unless diluted
