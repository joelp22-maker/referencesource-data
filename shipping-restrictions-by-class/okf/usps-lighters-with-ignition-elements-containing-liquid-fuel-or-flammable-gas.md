**Item category:** Lighters with ignition elements containing liquid fuel or flammable gas

**Carrier:** USPS

**Restriction type:** restricted

**Scope:** domestic mail via surface transportation only

**Conditions:** Mailable via surface transportation only with DOT certification, PCSC director written authorization, specific packaging and labeling; shipping paper not required

> Lighters with ignition elements and containing liquid fuel are classified as a Class 3 flammable liquid; those containing flammable gas are Division 2.1. Mailing is permitted only for domestic surface transportation only
