**Item category:** Hand sanitizer

**Carrier:** USPS

**Restriction type:** hazmat declaration required

**Conditions:** Listed by the Department of Transportation as posing a significant safety risk while being transported; $7.50 hazmat handling fee for Priority Mail; $50 noncompliance fee for packages not properly declared and labeled across all commercial packages including Ground Advantage (effective July 12 2026)
