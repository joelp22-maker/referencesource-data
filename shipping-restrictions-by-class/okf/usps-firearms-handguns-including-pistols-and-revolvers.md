**Item category:** firearms - handguns (including pistols and revolvers)

**Carrier:** USPS

**Restriction type:** restricted

**Scope:** domestic mail

**Conditions:** A firearm with a short stock designed to be held and fired by a single hand; includes pistols, revolvers, and firearms capable of being concealed on a person including short-barreled shotguns and short-barreled rifles

> Pistols, revolvers, and other firearms capable of being concealed on a person, including but not limited to, short-barreled shotguns and short-barreled rifles, are categorized as handguns.
