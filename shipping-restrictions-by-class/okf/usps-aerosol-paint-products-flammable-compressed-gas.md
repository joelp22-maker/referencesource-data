**Item category:** Aerosol paint products (flammable compressed gas)

**Carrier:** USPS

**Restriction type:** restricted

**Scope:** domestic mail via surface transportation only

**Conditions:** Acceptable via surface transportation only if qualifies as Limited Quantity ground material and meets quantity limitations and applicable packaging requirements

> Aerosol Paint Products: Aerosol paint products that are defined as flammable compressed gases are acceptable in the domestic mail via surface transportation only if they can qualify as a Limited Quantity ground material
