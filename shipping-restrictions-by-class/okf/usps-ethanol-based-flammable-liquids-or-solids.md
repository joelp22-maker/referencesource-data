**Item category:** Ethanol-based flammable liquids or solids

**Carrier:** USPS

**Restriction type:** restricted

**Scope:** domestic mail via air only

**Conditions:** Mailable by domestic air only with prior written authorization from director, Product Classification; quantity and packaging limits apply based on ethanol concentration

> Flammable liquids or solids containing ethanol (ethyl alcohol) are mailable by domestic air only with prior written authorization.
