**Item category:** Used, damaged, or defective electronic devices

**Carrier:** USPS

**Restriction type:** prohibited

**Scope:** international mail and domestic air transportation

**Conditions:** Prohibited from international mail and domestic air transportation; exceptions for new devices in original packaging or manufacturer-certified new/refurbished devices; surface transport must mark with 'Restricted Electronic Device' and 'Surface Transportation Only'

> Used, Damaged, or Defective Electronic Devices: Used, damaged, or defective electronic devices in international mail or domestic air transportation, except for new devices in original packaging or manufacturer-certified new or refurbished devices.
