**Conditions:** Air guns are not regulated as firearms unless manufactured with frames or receivers of an actual firearm; devices that expel projectiles using compressed air or gas including paintball and pellet guns

> Air guns are devices that expel projectiles using compressed air or other gas (including paintball and pellet guns). Air guns are not regulated as firearms unless they are manufactured with frames or receivers of an actual firearm.
