**Item category:** Lithium-ion (PI 965) cells or batteries packed alone — Section IB

**Carrier:** IATA (air)

**Restriction type:** restricted

**Scope:** international air transport

**Conditions:** Applies to lithium ion cells/batteries at or below threshold watt-hour content and within the quantity limit in Table 965-IB. Full IATA DGR compliance required (including initial and biennial training) EXCEPT that UN specification packaging is not required. Effective March 31, 2022 — Section II was removed.

> Section IB now applies solely to lithium cells or batteries with a lithium content at or below the threshold amount and in a quantity that does not exceed that allowed in Section IB, Table 965-IB or Section IB, Table 968-IB, respectively.
