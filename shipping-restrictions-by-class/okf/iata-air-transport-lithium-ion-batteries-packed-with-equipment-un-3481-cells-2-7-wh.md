**Item category:** Lithium ion batteries packed with equipment (UN 3481) — cells >2.7 Wh

**Carrier:** IATA air transport

**Restriction type:** restricted

**Scope:** air transport

**Conditions:** Starting January 1, 2026, must be offered for transport at a state of charge not exceeding 30% of their rated design capacity.

> Starting January 1, 2026 , shipments of the following must be offered for transport at a state of charge not exceeding 30% of their rated design capacity: UN 3481, lithium ion batteries packed with equipment where the cells/batteries have a Watt-hour rating greater than 2.7 Wh.
