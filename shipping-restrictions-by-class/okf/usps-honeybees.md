**Item category:** honeybees

**Carrier:** USPS

**Restriction type:** restricted

**Scope:** continental United States - surface transportation only

**Conditions:** Must be free of disease; acceptable to mail only via surface transportation; mailpieces must be plainly marked with 'Live Bees' and 'Surface Only' or 'Surface Mail Only'

> Honeybees: Acceptable to mail only via surface transportation (except for queen honeybees; refer to b.). Mailpieces must be plainly marked on the address side with "Live Bees" and "Surface Only" or "Surface Mail Only."
