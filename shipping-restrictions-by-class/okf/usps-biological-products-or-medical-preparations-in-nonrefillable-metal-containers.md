**Item category:** Biological products or medical preparations in nonrefillable metal containers

**Carrier:** USPS

**Restriction type:** restricted

**Scope:** domestic surface mail only

**Conditions:** Nonflammable solutions accepted for domestic surface mail only if they comply with Packaging Instruction 2F

> Biological Products or Medical Preparations: Nonflammable solutions containing biological or medical products in nonrefillable metal containers preparation that heat could deteriorate may be accepted for domestic surface mail only
