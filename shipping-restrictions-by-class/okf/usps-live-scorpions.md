**Item category:** live scorpions

**Carrier:** USPS

**Restriction type:** restricted

**Scope:** continental United States only - surface transportation only

**Conditions:** Scorpions are mailable only when sent for the purposes of medical research or the manufacturing of antivenin; must be sent by surface transportation within continental United States; must be properly packaged in double container system

> scorpions are mailable only when sent for the purposes of medical research or the manufacturing of antivenin. Scorpions are nonmailable under any other circumstances.
