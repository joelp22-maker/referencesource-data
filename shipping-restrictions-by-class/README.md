# Mailing and air-carriage restrictions by item category

What item categories may be mailed, and what may be carried by air, with the conditions that apply. The spine is the USPS domestic mailability list (Publication 52, Appendix A) — about 90% of records. Alongside it: UK postal (Royal Mail, Post Office, Parcelforce, Royal Mail Wholesale), the UPU prohibited-articles entries for the US and UK, and about 116 records that are not about mail at all but about US air transport — passenger baggage and air cargo under FAA PackSafe, TSA, IATA and ICAO packing instructions, and 49 CFR. All three private couriers are covered as of 2026-09-01: FedEx (the International Connect prohibited and restricted item lists), UPS (the US Tariff/Terms and Conditions of Service — the items not accepted for transportation, plus the commodity sections on alcohol, marijuana and hemp, biological substances, firearms and ammunition, dry ice, limited quantity packages, hazardous waste and mercury, live animals, perishables, portable electronic devices, and tobacco and vaping), and DHL Express US (the prohibited commodities and the items needing prior approval, from DHL's own US 'what can I ship' page). Answers 'can I mail a lighter', 'USPS lithium battery mailing rules', 'can I fly with a power bank' and 'is [item] prohibited or restricted by [carrier]'. The carrier and scope fields on each record say which question it answers: a rule about carry-on baggage is not a rule about mail.

**3,194 records.** Canonical, always-current version: [https://referencesource.org/shipping-restrictions-by-class/](https://referencesource.org/shipping-restrictions-by-class/)
