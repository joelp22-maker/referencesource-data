# US boiler and pressure vessel inspection requirements by state

State-by-state comparison of boiler and pressure vessel inspection requirements across all US states. Each record is one state with its inspection intervals by boiler type (power, low-pressure steam, hot water, unfired pressure vessel), accepted inspector qualifications (state, insurance, owner-user), residential and size/pressure exemptions, governing statute citation, and administering agency. Answers 'how often does Ohio require boiler inspections', 'which states accept insurance company inspections in lieu of state inspections', and 'are residential boilers exempt in my state'.

**55 records.** Canonical, always-current version: [https://referencesource.org/boiler-inspection-requirements-by-state/](https://referencesource.org/boiler-inspection-requirements-by-state/)
