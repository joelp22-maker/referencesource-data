# State drug price transparency reporting thresholds, triggers, and penalties for pharmaceutical manufacturers

State-by-state comparison of the dollar thresholds and percentage-increase triggers that require pharmaceutical manufacturers to report drug pricing information to state regulatory agencies. As of 2026, approximately 23 US states have enacted drug price transparency laws, each with different WAC (wholesale acquisition cost) thresholds for new drugs, different percentage-increase triggers for existing drugs, different reporting timeframes, and different penalty structures. Each record is one state with its reporting thresholds. Answers 'at what price or price increase must I report in [state]', 'what is the penalty for not reporting in [state]', and 'which states require drug price transparency reporting'.
