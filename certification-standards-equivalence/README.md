# Certification marks and hazardous-area classifications: cross-market equivalences

Which product-safety certification mark applies in which market, who administers it and under what law (CE, UL, CSA, CCC, PSE, KC, BSMI, RCM, EAC, NOM, INMETRO and others), and how the two hazardous-area classification systems map onto each other: NEC Class/Division designations against IEC/ATEX Zone designations, gas group cross-references (NEC Group A-D against IEC IIA-IIC), temperature-class mappings, and the protection-concept codes (Ex d, AEx e, intrinsic safety) with the US, Canadian and IEC standards behind each. Answers 'is a CB certificate accepted in the US', 'what is the ATEX equivalent of Class I Division 2', and 'which mark is compulsory in China' — questions where the honest answer is often that there is no direct equivalent, and saying so is the value.
