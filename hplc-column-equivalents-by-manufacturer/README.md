# HPLC column equivalents to other manufacturers' columns

Which replacement HPLC column a vendor offers for a named column from another manufacturer, keyed on the column being replaced. A lab whose method is built around a discontinued or hard-to-source column needs a same-selectivity substitute, and the column makers publish their own mappings from a competitor's product name to their own. Two such charts are merged here, and most of the table is one of them. GL Sciences' equivalent list supplies the bulk -- 597 rows across eight brands (Agilent, Waters, Phenomenex, Thermo Fisher, Sigma-Aldrich/Merck, YMC, Restek, Shimadzu), each row giving a direct replacement and an alternative. Waters' Column Comparison Chart adds 158 rows across seventeen brands, each row giving a bonded phase, a closest-selectivity column, and recommended UPLC, HPLC and preparative columns. Of 696 records, 538 carry GL Sciences' answer only, 99 carry Waters' answer only, and 59 carry both. Where both answer they do not agree, and are not meant to: each vendor recommends its own products, so a two-vendor record is two competing answers rather than one answer confirmed twice. What the merge adds over either vendor's own page is those 59 records, and the fact that the combined table runs both ways where each vendor's chart runs only outward -- GL Sciences answers for 106 Waters columns, Waters for 8 GL Sciences ones. For the other 637 records the vendor's own chart says the same thing and is the original.
