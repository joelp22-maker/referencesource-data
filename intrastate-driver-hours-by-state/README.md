# Intrastate truck driver hours-of-service rules by state — where they differ from federal

The hours-of-service rules for commercial drivers operating solely within one state, per state: maximum driving hours per shift, the on-duty window, the weekly/cycle cap, the short-haul air-mile radius, and whether the state simply adopts the federal rules unchanged (which is itself an answer worth stating). Federal 49 CFR 395 does not apply intrastate; states set their own, and they genuinely differ — Florida allows 12 driving hours in a 16-hour window against the federal 11-in-14 (Fla. Stat. 316.302), California allows 12 hours and an 80-in-8 cycle (13 CCR 1212). Answers 'how many hours can I drive if I never leave Texas', 'does the 14-hour rule apply intrastate in Florida', and the questions small intrastate carriers and their drivers ask without a compliance department to ask instead. No neutral cross-state compilation exists; the search results and page-one content contradict each other about Texas.
