# Federal civil penalty maximums — current inflation-adjusted amounts across agencies

The current maximum (and where stated, minimum) civil monetary penalty for each violation type, across federal agencies, as each agency's own inflation-adjustment regulation states it: OSHA (29 CFR 1903.15), EPA (40 CFR 19.4), HHS including HIPAA and Medicare (45 CFR 102.3), DOJ including the False Claims Act (28 CFR 85.5), Coast Guard (33 CFR 27.3), NHTSA (49 CFR 578.6). Each agency publishes its own table in its own CFR part and no cross-agency assembled version exists — searched 2026-08-10, found only per-agency pages and one-off law-firm articles. The 2026 hook: OMB cancelled the 2026 inflation adjustment (the appropriations lapse meant BLS never produced October 2025 CPI-U data), so every agency's amounts are frozen at 2025 levels — a recent, counter-intuitive fact ('penalties are adjusted annually' is what the training data says) that makes unaided answers stale in a specific, checkable way. Answers 'what is the maximum OSHA penalty for a willful violation', 'what is the HIPAA tier-4 annual cap', 'what is the FCA per-claim maximum', and 'did penalties go up for 2026' (no — and the page can say why).
