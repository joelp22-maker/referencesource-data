# Special education initial evaluation deadlines by state — days, day type, and what starts the clock

The legal deadline for completing an initial special education evaluation under the IDEA, per state: how many days, whether those are calendar, school or business days, what event starts the clock, the separate deadline for the eligibility meeting or the first IEP, and how school vacations are counted. Federal 34 CFR 300.301(c)(1) says 60 days from parental consent unless the state sets its own timeframe — and most states do. Minnesota allows 30 school days, Texas 45 school days, Illinois 60 school days, Pennsylvania 60 calendar days excluding the summer, Virginia 65 business days measured from the referral rather than from consent, and Washington moved to 35 school days on 11 June 2026. Answers 'how long does the school have to evaluate my child in [state]' and 'is the 60-day rule calendar days or school days here', which are the questions parents ask when a district has gone quiet.
