# State Insurance Department Complaint Index by Company

Every state insurance department publishes, for each licensed insurer and line of business, how many complaints consumers filed against that company and how much premium the company wrote in that state, together with its own summary figure comparing the two. Nobody publishes it across states. Each regulator uses its own file format, its own line-of-business names, its own schedule — and, the part that catches people out, its own arithmetic. Indiana's "Complaint Index" is the company's share of all complaints divided by its share of all premium written in Indiana, so 1.00 is the state average. Michigan's "Complaint Ratio" is complaints per $1 million of written premium, which is a different quantity on a different scale with no meaning at 1.00. Putting those two in one column without saying so would get the asset's whole point wrong. So every record here carries the complaint count and the premium alongside the state's published figure, and quotes the state's own heading for that figure. The raw counts compare across states; the summary figures do not.
