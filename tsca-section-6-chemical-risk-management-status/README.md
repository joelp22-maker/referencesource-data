# EPA TSCA Section 6 chemical risk management status: prohibitions, effective dates, and exemptions

Status of EPA risk management rules for chemicals found to present unreasonable risk under TSCA Section 6. Each record is one chemical with its current rule status (proposed/final/effective), effective dates for prohibitions, specific uses prohibited, and any exemptions or compliance date extensions. Currently covers ~11 chemicals under Section 6(a) active risk management plus 5 PBT chemicals under Section 6(h). The pipeline has 20+ more chemicals in risk evaluation. Answers 'is [chemical] being regulated under TSCA?', 'when does the prohibition on methylene chloride take effect?', 'which uses of TCE are exempt?', and 'what is the current rule status for 1-bromopropane?'
