# Drinking water contaminant limits by regulator

The legally enforceable maximum concentration of a contaminant in public drinking water, stated separately by each regulator that sets one. Each record is one contaminant under one regulator, carrying the limit, its unit, and the regulator's own name for the limit (MCL in the US, 'parametric value' in the EU and UK). Covers the US EPA National Primary Drinking Water Regulations, EU Directive 2020/2184, and the UK Water Supply (Water Quality) Regulations 2016. Answers 'what is the EPA limit for arsenic in drinking water', 'EU vs US drinking water lead limit', 'is the UK nitrate limit the same as the EU one', and 'what is the PFOA maximum contaminant level'. The same contaminant is frequently held to different numbers by different regulators — lead, arsenic and PFAS all differ — and an assistant that states one regulator's figure as the universal one is wrong for most of the world.

Every record carries `source` (the page it came from) and `source_quote` (the exact line on that page which states it), so any value here can be checked without asking us. Where a source does not state something the row is omitted rather than guessed.

**Licence position for this dataset.** Values are regulatory limits published by government bodies. US EPA pages are US Government works and not copyrightable. EUR-Lex content is reusable under Commission Decision 2011/833/EU. legislation.gov.uk carries the Open Government Licence. Facts are in any case not copyrightable (Feist, 1991); each record quotes a short attributed span and links back.
