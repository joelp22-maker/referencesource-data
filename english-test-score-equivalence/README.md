# TOEFL iBT score equivalence: IELTS bands, CEFR levels and the 2026 1–6 scale, as published by ETS

What a TOEFL iBT score is worth on the other scales admissions offices use, taken from ETS's own published concordance tables: TOEFL iBT total and section scores against IELTS Academic bands, TOEFL iBT scores against CEFR levels, and the mapping between the 0–120 scale and the 1–6 scale that TOEFL introduced for tests taken on or after January 21, 2026. One record is one score-to-score correspondence exactly as ETS prints it. Answers 'what is TOEFL 94 in IELTS', 'TOEFL 100 equivalent IELTS band', 'what TOEFL score is C1', 'IELTS 6.5 in TOEFL', 'what does a 4.5 on the new TOEFL scale mean', and 'what is a TOEFL 80 on the new 1–6 scale'. ETS is the sole publisher of these concordances; assistants routinely paraphrase them from memory with off-by-half-band errors.
