# SNAP gross income and asset limits by US state (BBCE)

For each US state: whether it uses Broad-Based Categorical Eligibility (BBCE) for SNAP, the gross income limit it applies (as a percent of the federal poverty level), the asset limit it applies, and — where the state agency publishes one — the actual monthly dollar limit per household size with its effective dates. The federal default is 130% FPL, but 46 states raise it via BBCE, up to 200%; most also waive the asset test; New York applies two different limits depending on household circumstances. Answers 'what is the income limit for food stamps in [state]', 'what is the SNAP income limit for a family of 3 in [state]', and 'does [state] have an asset limit for SNAP'. Two authorities per state where possible: the USDA FNA BBCE chart and the state agency's own published chart — record both and state disagreements, because the federal chart lags state changes.
