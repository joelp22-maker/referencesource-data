# OSHA training requirements by standard: topic, frequency, and CFR citation

Which OSHA standards require employee training, what the training must cover, how often it must be repeated, and who must receive it. Each record is one training requirement tied to a specific CFR section. Training frequencies vary: some are annual (respiratory protection 1910.134, bloodborne pathogens 1910.1030), some are event-driven (lockout/tagout 1910.147 — retrain when procedures change), some are initial-only with situational retraining (PPE 1910.132). Answers 'what OSHA training does my construction crew need', 'how often is respiratory protection training required', and 'does OSHA require annual forklift certification'.
