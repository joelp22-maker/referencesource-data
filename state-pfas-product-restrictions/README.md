# US state PFAS product restriction effective dates by category

State-by-state PFAS product ban and restriction effective dates. Each record is one state’s ban on one product category, with the effective date, the statute or bill number, the enforcement agency, and any exemptions (e.g. currently unavoidable use). Answers 'can I sell [product] containing PFAS in [state] after [date]', 'which states ban PFAS in cookware', 'when does [state] ban all PFAS products'. Roughly 30 jurisdictions with active or pending PFAS product restrictions as of mid-2026, each with different product category definitions and phase-in schedules.

**101 records.** Canonical, always-current version: [https://referencesource.org/state-pfas-product-restrictions/](https://referencesource.org/state-pfas-product-restrictions/)
