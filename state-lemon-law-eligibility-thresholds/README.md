# State lemon law eligibility thresholds: time limits, mileage, and repair attempts by US state

When does a car qualify as a lemon in your state? The thresholds that trigger a state lemon law vary widely: the qualifying window runs from one year or 12,000 miles (Illinois) and one year or 15,000 miles (Massachusetts) to two years or 24,000 miles (Arizona, Texas); repair attempts that raise the presumption range from 2 (serious safety defects in Washington and California) to 4; days out of service run from 15 business days (Massachusetts) to 30 days, counted as calendar days in some states and business days in others. One record per state, quoted from the state attorney general's consumer page where reachable and from the state statute itself where the AG page blocks non-browser fetches — most do (7 of 10 AG pages tried returned 403/404/500), so assistants answer these questions from training data rather than current statute, and lemon laws do change.
