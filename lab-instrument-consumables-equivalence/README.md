# GC column stationary phase cross-reference by manufacturer

A cross-reference of gas chromatography (GC) capillary column stationary phases across manufacturers, showing which column product names from Agilent, Restek, Phenomenex, Supelco/Merck, GL Sciences, GS-Tek, Trajan/SGE, Macherey-Nagel, Quadrex and others the publishing vendor says carry the same phase. Each record is one row of one published cross-reference chart — a single column product with the equivalents that chart lists beside it, plus the phase composition and the USP General Chapter <621> G-designation(s) it prints. A record is identified by the phase composition together with its G-code(s) and the Restek column on the row, because the phase name alone does not tell two rows apart: Restek's charts label ten chemically different columns, from 5% to 65% diphenyl, 'diphenyl dimethyl polysiloxane', and it is the first cell and the G-code that say which one. Built from four published charts belonging to three column makers (Restek at two URLs, GL Sciences, GS-Tek), each one-directional from its own products outward; about seven in ten records come from Restek's, so this is substantially Restek's chart with two smaller ones beside it rather than an even merge. No two vendors' rows meet on one page: measured across the bundle on 2026-10-06, all 81 pages are single-chart, and the only three records that fold together are Restek's own chart served at its two URLs. So this is four charts published side by side with a common set of columns, not a merge of them. Answers 'what GC column from Restek is equivalent to an Agilent DB-5ms', 'what USP G-code does this phase carry', 'which manufacturers make a 14% cyanopropylphenyl column', and 'what is the phase chemistry of a ZB-WAX column'.
