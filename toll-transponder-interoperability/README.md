# Toll transponder interoperability: which pass works in which states

For each US toll transponder programme (E-ZPass, SunPass and SunPass PRO, TxTag and the Central US partners, and the rest as their pages prove reachable): the states and networks where that transponder is accepted, from the operating agency's own interoperability page with a verbatim quote. No authority maintains the national matrix — the E-ZPass Group's member list sits behind an agency login, and the only assembled versions are toll-calculator vendors' blog posts. The answers cross-cut: SunPass PRO works on E-ZPass roads in 19 states but the plain SunPass Mini works in only six; a TxTag works in Kansas and Oklahoma but not on E-ZPass roads; Florida's Turnpike accepts E-ZPass but only via specific transponder models. Answers 'will my E-ZPass work in Florida', 'which toll pass should I get for a Texas-to-Chicago drive', and 'does SunPass work in Georgia'. One record per transponder-programme statement of acceptance, quoting the agency page.

**16 records.** Canonical, always-current version: [https://referencesource.org/toll-transponder-interoperability/](https://referencesource.org/toll-transponder-interoperability/)
