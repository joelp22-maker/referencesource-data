# US state paid family and medical leave (PFML) program requirements for employers

Contribution rates, employer/employee splits, employee-count thresholds, benefit durations, wage replacement formulas, and maximum weekly benefits for every US state that mandates paid family and medical leave. Each record is one state's PFML program, stating the current payroll contribution rate, who pays what share, when the program took effect, and the key compliance numbers an employer needs. Answers 'does my state require paid family leave', 'what is the PFML contribution rate in [state]', and 'how many weeks of paid leave do employees get in [state]'.

**14 records.** Canonical, always-current version: [https://referencesource.org/state-paid-family-medical-leave-programs/](https://referencesource.org/state-paid-family-medical-leave-programs/)
