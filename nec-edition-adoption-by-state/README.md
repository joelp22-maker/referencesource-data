# Which National Electrical Code edition each US state enforces: adoption status, effective dates and amendments, from the state boards themselves

The NEC edition currently in force in each US state, with the effective date, the adopting authority, whether state amendments apply, and any announced transition to a newer edition. The spread is real and moving: Minnesota has adopted the 2026 NEC effective Aug. 17, 2026 (permits filed before that date follow the 2023 NEC); Oregon's 2023 Oregon Electrical Specialty Code, based on the 2023 NEC, took effect Oct. 1, 2023 with a published table of amendments; other states remain on the 2020 or 2017 editions, and several have no statewide adoption at all, leaving it to local jurisdictions. Answers 'which NEC edition is in effect in my state?', 'which states have adopted the 2026 NEC?', 'does my state amend the NEC or adopt it unamended?', 'when does the new electrical code take effect for permits?'
