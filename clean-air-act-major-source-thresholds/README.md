# Clean Air Act "major source" emission thresholds by pollutant and area classification

Whether a stationary source (a factory, plant, or facility) needs a federal Title V or PSD/nonattainment-NSR air permit turns on whether its potential to emit crosses a tons-per-year (tpy) threshold, and the single definition in 40 CFR 70.2 sets a different number for each of three overlapping tests: the section 112 hazardous-air-pollutant test (10 tpy single HAP / 25 tpy combined), the section 302 major-stationary-source test (100 tpy of any regulated pollutant, or 100,000 tpy CO2e for greenhouse gases), and the section 182/188 nonattainment-area test, which is tiered by pollutant (VOC, NOx, CO, PM-10) and by how severely out of attainment the area is classified (Marginal/Moderate down to 100 tpy, Serious down to 50 tpy, Severe down to 25 tpy, Extreme down to 10 tpy for ozone precursors). A facility just under one threshold and just over another is a real, consequential distinction that the single defined term 'major source' hides. Each record is one pollutant/test/classification combination and its threshold. Answers 'what's the major-source threshold for VOC in a Severe ozone nonattainment area', 'does 25 tpy of a HAP make me major', 'what's the PSD threshold for a new pollutant'.
