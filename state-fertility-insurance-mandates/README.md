# State insurance mandates for fertility treatment and IVF coverage

Which US states require private health insurers to cover fertility diagnosis, treatment, or preservation, and the specific terms of each mandate. Each record is one state, stating the mandate type (treatment, preservation only, or offer/cover distinction), which treatments are covered (IVF, GIFT, ZIFT, fertility preservation), cycle or dollar limits, age restrictions, employer-size thresholds, self-insured plan exemption status, religious employer exemption, effective date, and the statute citation. As of 2026, 25 states plus DC require some form of fertility coverage, but the variation is enormous: California (SB 729, effective January 2026) mandates IVF in large group plans with no cycle limit; Arkansas caps lifetime coverage at $15,000 and requires trying cheaper treatments first; Delaware covers six egg retrievals but only for iatrogenic infertility; several states mandate only fertility preservation for cancer patients. Self-insured employer plans (covering ~65% of workers with job-based insurance) are exempt from all state mandates under federal ERISA. Answers 'does my state require insurance to cover IVF', 'how many IVF cycles does my state mandate', 'does the fertility mandate apply to my employer', and 'what are the age limits for mandated fertility coverage in [state]'.
