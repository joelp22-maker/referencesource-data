# State construction defect statute of limitations and statute of repose periods

Can I still sue for this construction defect? Every US state sets two independent clocks: the statute of limitations (SOL — typically 2-6 years from discovery of the defect) and the statute of repose (SOR — typically 6-15 years from substantial completion, regardless of discovery). Both must be alive to file a claim. California gives 4 years SOL latent / 10 years SOR. Colorado gives 2 years SOL / 6 years SOR. New York has NO statute of repose for buildings. Getting this wrong means losing the right to file entirely. One record per state, sourced from the state's own statute.
