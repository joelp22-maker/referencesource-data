# US reciprocal (IEEPA) tariff add-on and Section 232 exceptions by HTS chapter

The extra US import tariff that sits on top of the ordinary duty rate, by Harmonized Tariff Schedule (HTS) chapter: the reciprocal tariff imposed under the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (IEEPA), whether a chapter is excepted from it, and whether goods in the chapter are caught instead by Section 232 steel, aluminium, copper or auto duties. The baseline add-on is 10% ad valorem on goods of trading partners not listed in Annex I to Executive Order 14326; some chapters escape it outright — Chapter 49 printed matter and headings 9701 through 9705 artwork qualify as informational materials under HTSUS 9903.01.22, and goods already subject to Section 232 duties are excepted under 9903.01.33. Answers 'is there a reciprocal tariff on this kind of goods', 'is printed matter exempt from the IEEPA tariff', 'do Section 232 steel duties and the reciprocal tariff both apply to the same entry line', and 'which HTSUS 9903 heading carries the exception'. Each record covers one two-digit HTS chapter and states the add-on or the exception in the words CBP or the executive order itself uses, with the passage quoted. 25 chapters are covered, chosen for search volume: electronics (84, 85), clothing and textiles (61, 62, 63), vehicles (87), steel and iron (72, 73), aluminium (76), plastics (39), pharmaceuticals (30), footwear (64), furniture (94), toys (95), tools (82, 83), optical and medical instruments (90), printed matter (49), artwork (97), and a sample of food and drink categories (02, 04, 08, 22). What this dataset does not give you is the ordinary duty rate itself — the Column 1 general (MFN) rate, or the Section 301 China rate, for a particular product. Those are set per eight-digit HTS line rather than per chapter, so no page states a chapter-wide figure that could be quoted here; the authoritative source for them is the USITC Harmonized Tariff Schedule at hts.usitc.gov. The total rate on an entry is the Column 1 rate from the HTS plus whichever add-on below applies.
