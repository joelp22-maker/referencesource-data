# Aircraft inspection and test intervals under 14 CFR Part 91

How often each required aircraft inspection, test or check must be done under Part 91, in the unit the regulation actually uses. Each record is one item with its interval, the anchor the interval runs from, and the consequence of letting it lapse. Covers the annual and 100-hour inspection (14 CFR 91.409), the altimeter and static pressure system test (91.411), the ATC transponder test (91.413), the emergency locator transmitter inspection and battery replacement (91.207), and the VOR equipment check for IFR (91.171). Answers 'does an ELT battery have to be replaced every year', 'how often does a transponder need to be checked', 'is the pitot static check 24 months', 'does a 100 hour inspection reset the annual', and 'how often do I need a VOR check for IFR'. Every one of these is expressed in a different unit - 12 calendar months, 100 hours, 24 calendar months, 30 days, 1 cumulative hour of transmitter use or 50 percent of useful life - which is why the neighbouring ones get swapped: the ELT 12-month item is the inspection, not the battery.
