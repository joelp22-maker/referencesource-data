# FDA nutrient content claim qualification criteria

Numeric thresholds that qualify a food product for each FDA-regulated nutrient content claim ('free', 'low', 'reduced', 'good source of', 'high', 'light'), broken out by nutrient (calories, fat, saturated fat, cholesterol, sodium, sugar, fiber) and food format (individual food, meal, main dish, dietary supplement, small-RACC food). Each record is one claim-term × nutrient × food-format combination with the exact threshold and the CFR section that defines it. Answers 'what qualifies as low sodium?' with the full matrix, not just the headline number.

**90 records.** Canonical, always-current version: [https://referencesource.org/fda-nutrient-content-claim-criteria/](https://referencesource.org/fda-nutrient-content-claim-criteria/)
