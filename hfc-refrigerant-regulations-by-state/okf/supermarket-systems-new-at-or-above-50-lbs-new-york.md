**Equipment category:** Supermarket Systems (New), at or above 50 lbs

> New York has also finalized a new rulemaking under <https://dec.ny.gov/regulatory/regulations/proposed-emergency-recently-adopted-regulations/climate-change> NYCRR Part 494 which uses the 20-year GWP value rather than the traditional 100-year GWP value.
