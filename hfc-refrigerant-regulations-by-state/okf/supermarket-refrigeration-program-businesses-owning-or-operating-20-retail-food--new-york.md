**State:** New York

**Equipment category:** Supermarket Refrigeration Program (businesses owning or operating ≥ 20 retail food facilities)

**GWP limit:** GWP20 of < 10

**Effective date:** January 1, 2035

**Regulatory status:** signed into law

**Regulation citation:** NYCRR Part 494

> Additionally, the rule includes a Supermarket Refrigeration Program under which all businesses owning or operating ≥ 20 retail food facilities with a refrigerant charge capacity of ≥ 200 pounds in New York OR that operates > 100 such facilities in the US must meet one of the following conditions by January 1, 2035: All refrigerants used in supermarket systems containing ≥ 200 lbs of refrigerant have a GWP20 of < 10.
