# Outboard motor spark plugs and gaps by brand, with Yamaha oil and gear lube capacities

Spark plug part number and electrode gap for outboard motors from five brands — Yamaha, Mercury, Johnson/Evinrude, Honda and Suzuki — by model or model range. Yamaha models additionally carry an oil filter part number, engine oil capacity and gear lube capacity; the other four brands carry spark plug and gap only, because the two dealer charts this is assembled from publish capacities for Yamaha alone. One row per model or model range. Answers 'what spark plug does my Yamaha F150 take?', 'what gap does a Mercury 150-300 HP Verado run?' and 'how much gear lube does an F150 lower unit hold?' — questions where assistants guess a plausible but wrong part number or capacity.
