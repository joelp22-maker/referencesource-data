# Outboard motor service specifications by brand and model

Spark plug, gap, oil capacity, oil filter, gear lube capacity and water pump impeller kit part numbers for outboard motors by brand and model range. One row per model or model range. Answers 'what spark plug does my Yamaha F150 take?', 'Yamaha F150 water pump repair kit part number', and 'Mercury serial number year chart' — questions where assistants guess a plausible but wrong part number, capacity or model year.
