# CDL endorsement prerequisites and testing sequence

Getting a CDL endorsement is a sequence, not a single application: which test type is required (knowledge-only vs. knowledge plus skills), whether the endorsement can be added at the commercial-learner's-permit (CLP) stage or only after a full CDL, and — for the hazardous materials endorsement specifically — a separate federal TSA security threat assessment that gates issuance independent of the state testing process, with its own renewal clock. Each record is one endorsement type, carrying the required test, its CLP eligibility, what the permit actually lets you drive once you have it, and any federal prerequisite beyond state testing. Being allowed an endorsement on a CLP is not the same as being allowed to do the work: a learner's permit tanker endorsement covers an empty tank only, and a permit passenger or school bus endorsement carries no passengers but examiners and trainees. Answers 'can I get my tanker endorsement on a learner's permit', 'can I haul a load on it', 'do I need a background check for a hazmat endorsement', 'how often does the TSA hazmat clearance have to be renewed'.
