# US federal chemical regulatory reporting thresholds by program (CERCLA, EPCRA, CAA)

Cross-program comparison of federal regulatory thresholds for hazardous chemicals. Each record is one chemical with the thresholds that trigger obligations under different federal programs: CERCLA reportable quantity (release reporting to the National Response Center), EPCRA Section 302 threshold planning quantity and Section 304 reportable quantity (emergency planning and release notification), CAA Section 112(r) threshold quantity (risk management plan), and the lowered EPCRA Section 313 reporting threshold that applies to chemicals of special concern. The product is the cross-reference: chlorine has a CERCLA reportable quantity of 10 lb, an EPCRA threshold planning quantity of 100 lb, and a CAA Section 112(r) threshold quantity of 2,500 lb — three programs, three different numbers, on one page. Answers 'what are all my reporting obligations for [chemical]', 'is [chemical] an extremely hazardous substance under EPCRA', 'is [chemical] covered by the risk management plan rule', and 'what is the reportable quantity for [chemical]'. Section 313's ordinary reporting threshold is deliberately not a field here: 40 CFR 372.25 sets it programme-wide at 25,000 lb manufactured or processed and 10,000 lb otherwise used, so it is the same number for every chemical. Only the lowered thresholds in 40 CFR 372.28 vary by chemical, and those are the ones carried.

**1,340 records.** Canonical, always-current version: [https://referencesource.org/chemical-regulatory-reporting-thresholds/](https://referencesource.org/chemical-regulatory-reporting-thresholds/)
