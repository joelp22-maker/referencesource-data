**Brand:** Bosch

**Appliance type:** Dishwasher

**Error code:** E16 and E17

**Meaning:** There is an issue with water flowing into the dishwasher or the filling system. The water may not be able to enter the dishwasher.

> E16 and E17 There is an issue with water flowing into the dishwasher or the filling system. The water may not be able to enter the dishwasher. Check the inlet hose and make sure it isn't kinked or damaged. For further assistance on how to troubleshoot your dishwasher, contact Bosch Customer Support at 1-800-944-2904 .
