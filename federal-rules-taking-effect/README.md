# Federal rules taking effect: every published US final rule whose effective date is still in the future

Every final rule already published in the Federal Register whose effective date has not yet arrived, with the agency, the CFR parts it amends, the effective date as the rule prints it, and the rule's verbatim DATES paragraph. Answers 'which federal regulations take effect on January 1, 2027', 'what rules affecting 47 CFR part 10 are already published but not yet in force', 'what is the compliance date for the DOJ web accessibility rule', and 'what changes are coming for my industry that are already law'. The hard part is that a rule published in 2023 can carry a 2026 or 2033 effective date: the obligation is already binding law, it is nowhere near the front page, and the only way to find it is to know it exists. Measured 2026-08-10: 234 published final rules have effective dates on or after 2026-08-11, spread from September 2026 to March 2033.

Every record carries `source` (the page it came from) and `source_quote` (the exact line on that page which states it), so any value here can be checked without asking us. Where a source does not state something the row is omitted rather than guessed.

**Licence position for this dataset.** Federal Register documents are US government works in the public domain. The API is offered without a key and without registration; each record links back to the rule's own page on federalregister.gov.
